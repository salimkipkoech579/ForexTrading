Forex Trading Simulator Guide

This platform is an educational simulation designed to practice currency trading mechanics without financial risk.

What is a Pip?

A pip (Percentage in Point) is the smallest standardized unit of price movement for currencies (usually 0.0001 for EUR/USD, 0.01 for USD/JPY).

Lot Sizes & Leverage

1 standard lot equals 100,000 units of currency. Leverage (e.g. 1:100) allows you to control a position with only 1% initial margin deposit.
